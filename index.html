Introduction

The bond between humans and dogs transformed both species. Domesticated dogs inherited social instincts from wolves, aiding humans by alerting them to danger and enhancing social cohesion. Their versatility allowed them to assist in hunting, transport, guarding, herding, and waste management while providing companionship in isolated settings. In return, humans provided food, shelter, and protection, creating a partnership that thrived in harsh environments, potentially pivotal for survival. Some researchers suggest that dog domestication may have played a role in Homo sapiens’ success over Neanderthals, highlighting its significance.

How Dogs Help us

Dogs also have incredible abilities that allow them to help people in many different ways:

  • Guide dogs - Help people who are blind or visually impaired navigate their surroundings.
  • Service dogs - Assist people with disabilities with different everyday tasks.
  • Police and security dogs - Use their powerful sense of smell to assist police and security services.
  • Search and rescue dogs - Help locate people during emergencies and difficult situations.

Dogs with Special Skills

  1. German Sheperd - commonly trained for police, security and assistance work.
  2. Labrador Retriever – commonly trained as guide and assistance dogs.
